ThreadUTC
    How to read the chart

    The rows share a UTC clock. Scroll up and down through threads, and horizontally to pan when zoomed in. Drag the minimap to select a range, or use its arrow keys to pan when zoomed in. You can also drag the UTC axis. At six hours or less, select a thread to see its recorded events below the chart, timed from its first message. Brown can include time when a workflow ran alongside other work. Unclear intervals blend with nearby colors. A blended interval can look like the workflow around it. Very short slices merge at this zoom level. X1 also uses reviewed phase boundaries. The other nine rows use captured timestamps.
